HOPE privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

HOPE is a private personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner. This policy explains what HOPE does with Google account data.

What HOPE accesses

Only the owner's own Gmail accounts, and only after the owner signs in and agrees on Google's consent screen. HOPE asks for read-only access (the gmail.readonly permission), so it can read and search email but cannot send, delete, move, label or otherwise change anything.

How the data is used

Only to answer the owner's own requests, for example "any unread emails from my energy supplier?" or "what does the latest email from work say?". HOPE does not read email in the background, build profiles, show advertising, or use email for anything else.

Where the data goes

  • Email is fetched from Google directly to the owner's computer when the owner asks.
  • To answer, the relevant parts (for example senders, subjects and the text of a message the owner asks about) are sent to the AI language-model service HOPE uses, which is asked not to store or train on it. Nothing else is sent anywhere.
  • Email is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

What is stored

HOPE keeps a Google access token for each connected account, on the owner's computer only, so it can keep reading without asking the owner to sign in each time. It does not keep copies of emails; recent conversation with the owner may mention them and stays on that computer.

Removing access

The owner can remove HOPE's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete HOPE's saved tokens from their computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

HOPE's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email address shown on HOPE's Google sign-in (consent) screen.
