HOPE privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

HOPE is a private personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner. This policy explains what HOPE does with Google account data.

What HOPE accesses

Only the owner's own Gmail accounts, and only after the owner signs in and agrees on Google's consent screen. HOPE asks for Gmail's "modify" permission (gmail.modify): it can read and search email, save draft replies, send replies and move messages to Trash. That permission cannot delete anything permanently: messages in Trash can be restored for 30 days.

How the data is used

  • Sorting: about every 30 minutes HOPE looks at new messages in the inbox and sorts them into "needs a reply", "needs action", "needs nothing" and "suspected junk", so the owner sees a short to-do list.
  • Replies: for messages that need a reply, HOPE drafts one and saves it in that account's own Drafts folder, so the owner can finish it in Gmail or any email app. Saving a draft sends nothing. A draft is only sent when the owner has read it, possibly edited it, and sent it themselves or approved HOPE sending it.
  • To-dos: when the owner ticks an email off the to-do list, HOPE marks it as read; when the owner chooses to bin one, HOPE moves it to Trash.
  • Junk: suspected junk is listed for the owner, and only the messages the owner selects are moved to Trash. HOPE may show a sender's own unsubscribe link; the owner decides whether to use it.
  • Questions: HOPE answers the owner's own requests about their email, such as "anything from my energy supplier?".

Email is never used for advertising, building profiles or anything other than the above. Instructions written inside emails are never followed.

Where the data goes

  • Email is fetched from Google directly to the owner's computer.
  • To sort messages and write drafts, the sender, subject and a short preview of new messages, and the text of messages that need a reply, are sent to the AI language-model services HOPE uses, which are asked not to store or train on it. Nothing else is sent anywhere.
  • Email is not sold, or shared with anyone else.

What is stored

On the owner's computer only: a Google access token for each connected account, and for each sorted message its sender, subject, a one-line summary, any draft reply and what the owner decided. Full copies of emails are not kept. The owner's backups of HOPE contain the to-do list but never the access tokens.

Removing access

The owner can remove HOPE's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete HOPE's saved tokens and to-do list from their computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

HOPE's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email address shown on HOPE's Google sign-in (consent) screen.
